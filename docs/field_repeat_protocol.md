# Repeated field adaptation protocol

All 15 runs use the same **149-image field test cohort**, separated from a 150-image support pool by predefined visual duplicate clusters. Budgets 0/10/25/50/100 are nested within each of seeds 42/43/44. All greenhouse plants remain training data. Test images never select hyperparameters or epochs.

A fixed SHA-256 exact-duplicate and 64-bit pHash Hamming-distance <=6 audit identified 0 candidate pairs. Visual clusters are not recovered physical plant identities and do not guarantee plant-independent field evaluation. Candidate pairs and split manifests are retained. This is internal single-site domain transfer, not external validation. The field cohort lacks Davis grades 4 and 9.

Variation across the three seeds combines support-sample and training randomness; it does not estimate external-site or biological-cluster uncertainty. Present individual runs and descriptive dispersion, not strong inferential CIs from only three seeds.
